# Beyond Human Emotion: The Possibility of Distinct Artificial Emotional Systems
This paper is not about "how we engineer emotions into AI" or arguing that "AI already has feelings."

Instead, this paper explores the fundamental distinctions between human emotion and the emotional systems of other biological or synthetic organisms in our universe. To assume that all emotional architectures must mirror our own is a category error; the notion that future artificial intelligence will experience emotions equivalent to human consciousness is unfounded.
